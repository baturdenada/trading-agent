# Compare Multiple Trading Symbols

## Step 1: Get Data for Symbol 1
- action: get_candles
- symbol: XAUUSD.s
- days: {{days}}
- output: symbol1_data

## Step 2: Get Data for Symbol 2
- action: get_candles
- symbol: XAGUSD.s
- days: {{days}}
- output: symbol2_data

## Step 3: Get Data for Symbol 3
- action: get_candles
- symbol: EURUSD.s
- days: {{days}}
- output: symbol3_data

## Step 4: Analyze All Symbols
- action: ai_summary
- input: |
  Symbol 1 (XAUUSD): {{symbol1_data}}
  Symbol 2 (XAGUSD): {{symbol2_data}}
  Symbol 3 (EURUSD): {{symbol3_data}}
  
  Analysis Period: {{days}} days
  TP: {{tp}} | SL: {{sl}}
- prompt: |
  Compare these three symbols over the {{days}} day period.
  For each symbol:
  1. Identify the trend (up/down/sideways)
  2. Calculate price range
  3. Rate tradability (1-10)
  
  Which symbol is most profitable with TP {{tp}} and SL {{sl}}?
  Provide a ranking and brief reasoning.
- output: comparison_analysis

## Step 5: Create Comparison Report
- action: write_file
- path: C:\trading-memory\obsidian-vault\Comparison_{{date}}.md
- content: |
  # Symbol Comparison Report
  **Date:** {{date}}
  **Period:** {{days}} days
  
  ## Trading Parameters
  - Take Profit: {{tp}} points
  - Stop Loss: {{sl}} points
  
  ## Analysis
  {{comparison_analysis}}
  
  ## Recommendation
  Based on the analysis above, trade the top-rated symbol first.
  Monitor performance and adjust as needed.
  
  ---
  *Generated by Workflow Engine*
